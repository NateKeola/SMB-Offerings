# Vision and operating preferences

Recorded 2026-11-03. This is the user's project direction, summarized rather than a transcript. [Decisions and unresolved choices](decision-register.md) distinguish it from research proposals.

## The business thesis

Take something **proven**, make it **better**, and use something **new** when that enables the improvement. Existing demand and repeated workflow pain take priority over novelty. A compelling video is a reason to investigate a pattern; it is not evidence that customers buy it.

The intended business offers practical improvements to longstanding SMBs: easier product discovery, clearer inquiry and quote flows, useful websites, search foundations and bounded upkeep. AI can help research and implementation, but the offer should explain what a customer can do more easily. A conventional form, clearer navigation or configuring an existing tool may be the best solution.

The initial concrete example was a wholesale garment catalog where buyers struggle to describe and find the right product. This motivates natural-language discovery and quote preparation, not a claim that a particular distributor loses money or lacks semantic search. The reusable core should support different industries through specific profiles. One universal pitch will miss meaningful differences in buyers, data and buying processes.

## What an opportunity should look like

Input: an explicit ideal customer profile (ICP), geography, company criteria, allowed sources, exclusions and a bounded review budget.

Output: a verified business website or an honestly unresolved web presence, source-backed pain evidence, confidence and missingness, public business contact routes, a verified decision role when available, the next action, and a specific prototype offer. A list of registrations or attractive websites is only a starting point.

Prioritize recurring friction, accessible and usable data, a plausible buyer, delivery capacity and an outcome that can be measured. Consider whether an existing tool already solves the issue. Fully booked firms may need routing or administration improvements rather than more inquiries.

## Research preferences

- Use official city, county and open-government data where permitted for low-cost discovery. Preserve licensing and snapshot dates; verify operation and identity independently.
- Explore Instagram and YouTube creation/workflow examples with exact links, publication dates, observed metrics and review coverage. Recent capability evidence matters, but keep older useful fundamentals labeled as such.
- Look for repetition across independent sources. Reposts, same-creator examples and shared promotional origins do not count as independent corroboration.
- Engagement and follower counts help prioritize investigation. Keep rounded, hidden, unavailable and unknown values distinct. They are not a credibility score.
- Reddit is a desired future source of customer-language evidence; an approved commercial access route has not been established and the current evidence ledger has zero community observations.
- Use source-checked SEO and AI-search guidance. Optimize useful information and qualified inquiries, without promised rankings, citations or magic files.
- Explain any proposed download first. Avoid adding paid tools, data services or integrations before a small manual sample establishes the need.

## Sales and delivery preferences

Prepare a calling-first, multichannel approach: human calls, email drafts, LinkedIn and occasional in-person drop-ins. Execution stays human-controlled, and email sending is currently on hold. No channel activity is implied by research or a cadence template.

Show a prospect a small, honest example: what exists, what it does, what remains simulated, why the task may matter, price assumptions, setup effort and next validation. Avoid large speculative builds for every lead. Only call something built after the artifact and stated behavior have been checked.

Favor a low entry price, a menu of bounded improvements and recurring services with clear ongoing obligations. The $10/$30/$40 ideas are price experiments, not proven profitable standalone plans. Integration, maintenance, support, acquisition and paid usage must be counted. Use technical discovery and a statement of work before promising broad delivery.

## How to work together

Keep a durable, visible record of requested capabilities, current work and next steps. Put useful details into the main artifact rather than requiring the user to reconstruct them from hidden notes or scattered conversations. Keep reporting candid about what was tested and what remains unknown. Continue authorized work; do not mistake a research plan for permission to contact people, purchase services or launch production changes.
