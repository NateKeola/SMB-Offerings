# Scoped service menu

These are **offer and price hypotheses**, not delivered client work, validated demand or accepted quotes. The original [service research](https://github.com/NateKeola/Dogfood/blob/5bbe43b32bb8941106c5505c92f438bd4e4e0fe0/research/smb-service-opportunities/service-menu.md) was reviewed November 2, 2026. The user has not chosen a final ICP or price list.

Assume one experienced builder, one supported CMS/static stack, approved copy/assets, one reviewer and one revision round. Hours mean hands-on effort; access, approvals and DNS can add elapsed time. Taxes, paid media, platform/data charges and exceptional support are separate. Inspect the stack and data before quoting.

## Small entry offers

| Offer | Bounded deliverable and acceptance | Planning effort / price | Main exclusions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task diagnostic | Up to ten public pages, one inquiry/call path, three prioritized findings and a reproducible walkthrough | 1–2 hours; $99 once, optionally credited toward a $399 repair within 30 days | Implementation and promised traffic/lead gains |
| Quote-request repair | One existing page/form, up to three defects; mobile, keyboard, errors, basic abuse protection and authorized receipt test | 4–6 hours; $399 total experiment | Booking/payments/CRM replacement; unsupported stack requires re-estimate |
| Simple web presence | Up to three pages, one inquiry form, approved facts/assets, responsive and search basics | 8–16 hours; $999–$1,499 setup hypothesis | Large migration, custom commerce and unlimited revisions |
| Website care | One site's agreed checks, a monthly manual form-path review, one small edit and change log | 1–3 hours onboarding; $149/month experiment with 20 total human minutes/month | No rollover, unlimited support or 24/7 response; two-business-day response target, resolution may need a new quote |

Care's proposed weekly checks and monthly review are service scope ideas, not active automations. Care onboarding plus the repair must fit within the modeled six-hour setup allowance or be separately quoted. A $99 care option needs a reduced scope of at most ten human minutes/month and evidence that economics work. Sell a one-off fix if the customer has no useful recurring need.

## Bounded additions to an existing care account

| Idea | Included recurring obligation | Setup hypothesis | Monthly experiment |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Extra endpoint monitoring | One endpoint, failure/recovery alerts, no sensitive payloads; two average human minutes/month, remediation excluded | $49 / 0.5–1 hour | +$10 |
| Local-facts check | One location; hours, service area, contact facts and visible markup; up to 30 minutes per quarter | $99 / 1–2 hours | +$30 |
| Approved FAQ upkeep | Up to two short answer changes/month, 20 total minutes; static, owner-approved answers | $99 / 1–2 hours | +$40 |

These assume a shared invoice and account overhead. Do not charge again for a check or edit already included in care. Provider costs and support tails can erase the margin. Pause optional work and scope an upgrade if a cap is exceeded; do not silently charge overages. A one-time file installation is not inherently a retainer.

## Larger or uncertain offers

| Offer | Prototype and production boundary | Planning range / price experiment |
|---|---|---|
| Performance and search foundation | Up to ten pages: indexability, titles, links, sitemap, images and appropriate markup; measured before/after checks | 4–8 hours; $399–$799 once; $39/month quarterly review only if distinct from care |
| Search-focused service page | One useful page from real services/customer questions; approved local facts and one review round | 3–6 hours; $299–$599 once |
| AI-search observation pilot | Ten intents across two permitted surfaces; versioned query panel and variability/coverage report | 3–6 hours, $299–$599 setup; 1–3 hours plus tools/month, $199–$399 recurring hypothesis |
| Read-only catalog finder / quote draft | Local 50-item fictional catalog first; production scoped after data review to one supplier, up to 1,000 products and agreed variants | 12–24-hour prototype, $750–$1,500; additional 24–60-hour integration, $2,500–$6,000 hypothesis |
| Catalog upkeep | Freshness checks, failed imports, vendor changes and bounded support | At least 1–3 hours/month plus hosting/API; $249–$499 only after measured upkeep |
| Human-reviewed rep assistant | Offline grounded-answer demo; production adds permissions, evaluation, logs, limits and monitoring | 16–32-hour prototype, $1,500–$3,000; another 40–100 hours for production; recurring price separately validated |

Catalog acceptance should use about twenty buyer-defined find/filter tasks, source-linked facts and explicit stale-data behavior. Stock, compatibility and customer prices need authorized current sources. A rep assistant must handle unknowns, stale facts, unauthorized prices and hostile document instructions; it cannot send autonomously. Its scope is additional to catalog search, not included in that estimate.

These prices are independent scope experiments, not a combined package. The prototype archive uses a separate illustrative $75/hour planning rate; the economics model uses $60/hour delivery cost. Do not treat either as a market benchmark or silently combine the models.
