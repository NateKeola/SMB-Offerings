# Six original prototype directions

These summarize the [reviewed cluster records](https://github.com/NateKeola/Dogfood/blob/ebfbd71319364fe803b82bbd7af7e58434d667e5/projects/idea-archive/data/clusters.json). All are **brief-ready**, with zero built customer demos. Prices use an illustrative $75/hour planning rate, one review round, approved facts and owned/licensed assets. They are not market prices, a client quote or proof of demand. Production integrations, hosting, domains and ongoing support are excluded unless separately scoped.

The separate [service menu](service-menu.md) uses different scopes and price experiments; do not combine estimates as if they were one approved package.

## Show the result. Make the next step clear.

**Stable cluster ID:** `service-quote-path`.

**Customer/task hypothesis:** A local service owner with validated enquiry friction and capacity for appropriate jobs.

**Original scope:** A static service page with three service cards, owned before/after media, coverage details and a local quote-outline demo.

**Value hypothesis:** Visitors may understand services and prepare better enquiries when results, coverage, scope and next steps are clear.

**Planning effort:** 8–14 hours; $600–$1,050 at the illustrative $75/hour. Validate scope, dependencies and buyer value before quoting.

**Next validation:** Test whether three representative buyers can identify the right service and complete a fictional enquiry without help.

## Make the visit or enquiry effortless.

**Stable cluster ID:** `local-food-page`.

**Customer/task hypothesis:** A food, venue or visitor-service business with verified visitor-information or enquiry friction.

**Original scope:** An original café, restaurant or visitor-service page with readable menu or service details, location, a local enquiry action and optional motion with a static fallback.

**Value hypothesis:** Readable, accurate essentials may remove avoidable questions and help a visitor choose a next step.

**Planning effort:** 6–12 hours; $450–$900 at the illustrative $75/hour. Validate scope, dependencies and buyer value before quoting.

**Next validation:** Verify business facts, then test finding an item, opening hours and the inquiry route with motion disabled.

## Show proof the owner can keep current.

**Stable cluster ID:** `maintainable-service-portfolio`.

**Customer/task hypothesis:** A studio or service operator with authentic work to show and a verified difficulty maintaining useful project information.

**Original scope:** A three-page static service/portfolio design system with editable project data and a local inquiry demo.

## Research useful pages before making them.

**Stable cluster ID:** `local-intent-worksheet`.

**Customer/task hypothesis:** A local operator with an observed gap between real customer questions and useful service/location information.

**Original scope:** A local worksheet for services, intent, page gaps, questions, evidence dates and grouped page outlines.

**Value hypothesis:** An evidence-linked worksheet may focus effort on genuinely useful pages rather than thin location variants.

**Planning effort:** 4–8 hours; $300–$600 at the illustrative $75/hour. Validate scope, dependencies and buyer value before quoting.

**Next validation:** Check five fictional questions for evidence quality and whether they map to useful pages without duplication.

## Find a real problem before offering a concept.

**Stable cluster ID:** `qualification-before-concept`.

**Customer/task hypothesis:** A service provider evaluating fit, need, decision-making and capacity before a separately authorized pilot.

**Original scope:** A local qualification worksheet, clearly labeled concept outline and measured-pilot plan; no sending or dialing.

**Value hypothesis:** Qualification and honest concept labeling may prevent unsuitable pitches and clarify what should be validated next.

**Planning effort:** 4–8 hours; $300–$600 at the illustrative $75/hour. Validate scope, dependencies and buyer value before quoting.

**Next validation:** Use fictional scenarios including no need, fully booked and uncertain fit; verify that the workflow can disqualify them.

## Let visitors evaluate a space.

**Stable cluster ID:** `permissioned-property-viewer`.

**Customer/task hypothesis:** A property or venue operator with a verified need for remote space evaluation and permission to use the sample.

**Original scope:** A one-sample local viewer evaluation with room navigation, accessible fallback images and a fictional enquiry action.

**Value hypothesis:** A usable spatial preview may answer questions before an enquiry; test whether it improves on a simple image walkthrough.

**Planning effort:** 16–28 hours; $1,200–$2,100 at the illustrative $75/hour. Validate scope, dependencies and buyer value before quoting.

**Next validation:** Compare a permissioned sample viewer with a still-image walkthrough on mobile; record task success, loading, capture gaps, licensing and hosting cost.

**Source references:**
- [Explore a space before the enquiry](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DYFGThBOqRJ/) — unknown-origin.

**Limits:** One source, no independently verified origin, implementation or demand. Do not infer a market trend or ease of delivery.

Capture, model preparation, imagery licensing and hosting are separate unknowns. Compare with a simple still-image walkthrough before committing to a 3D workflow.
