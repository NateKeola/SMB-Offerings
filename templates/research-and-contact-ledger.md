# Private research and contact ledger — blank template

Proposed field contract, updated 2026-11-08. Complete real records only in an approved private system. This public template contains no leads, contacts, private source links, communications or permission grants. Do not populate it in Git.

## Batch and business record

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Batch ID / profile version | Scope, owner, date, geography definition, included services/products and caps |
| Business / branch ID | Stable identifier; legal/trading-name relationship and branch distinction |
| Located in / serves | Separate values with evidence and unknown states |
| Identity / operation | Official domain and source-backed association; current-operation/license checks where relevant |
| Discovery provenance | Source URL, checked date, provider/query context, allowed reuse/storage and expiry |
| Website / contact status | Observed present, no route found in specified checks, conflicting, unavailable or unknown; never infer absence from one field |
| Website / public business phone | Verified values, source URLs, business/branch association and checked date; unresolved fields stay explicit rather than fabricated |
| Revenue / sales evidence, when available | Value/range, currency, fiscal period, business/parent/branch scope, source URL/publisher and publication/check date; reported, estimated, disputed or unknown; visible methodology and limits |
| Deduplication | Match signals, linked source records, reviewed merges and reversibility |
| Status | Research stage, hold/disqualification reason, next verification and owner |

Current textile-wholesale target: 100 distinct businesses across California, Oregon, Washington and Arizona, with Los Angeles first and Texas later. Report discovery, deduplication, assessment and qualification counts separately. No state quota is implied. Keep unsupported revenue unknown; traffic/headcount estimates are not actual sales. All populated rows remain private.

## Evidence and opportunity record

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Evidence ID / origin | Direct observation, user report, source claim, inference or synthetic fixture |
| Task and method | Exact steps, expected result, actual result, viewport/device and checked time |
| Search snapshot | Query, location context, surface, ads/Maps/organic distinction, inspected depth and observed position if available |
| Source and limits | Exact relevant page or permitted private artifact; rights, freshness and coverage limits |
| Finding | Observed working/friction, not observed, unknown, not assessable or not applicable with reason |
| Reproduction / control | Known-working route and second dated observation; do not silently count one run twice |
| Interpretation | Narrow possible consequence, competing explanation and confidence reason |
| Scores | Existing rubric/version, individual checks, coverage and withheld/provisional status; not buying intent |
| Offer / buyer validation | Proposed fix, owner-confirmed pain or unknown, existing alternatives and data/integration needs |
| Review | Reviewer, exact record/evidence revisions, decision, expiry and reasons |

Use the [full opportunity record](opportunity-record.md) for demonstration scope, pricing assumptions and delivery questions. Evidence belongs to its observation date; a later edit does not rewrite history.

## Contact route

Store a public business phone, generic business inbox, business contact page or verified professional profile only when needed and permitted. For each route capture its exact source, association to the business/branch, checked date, role evidence, confidence and intended purpose. Keep generic business routes distinct from named people's details. Owner/founder status must be sourced; do not guess an email pattern or infer authority from a shared name.

If social-platform or account-based research is used, check permitted access and export/reuse terms first. The user having a work subscription does not establish that employer-owned contacts, exports, playbooks or account resources may be reused. No automated connections, messages or invitations are implied.

## Activity log

Append one entry for each actual event; keep planned tasks separate:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Activity ID / business / contact | Stable links and responsible person |
| Occurred at / time zone | Actual timestamp, or explicitly approximate user-reported time |
| Channel and actor | Human call, incoming reply, authorized message, private note or other actual event |
| Action state | Planned, draft, attempted, confirmed sent, delivery unknown, delivered, answered or completed, as supported |
| Evidence | Provider result, private source or user report; uncertainty preserved |
| Outcome | No answer, voicemail, useful conversation, not interested, wrong route, do not contact, follow-up requested, pilot discussion or other sourced outcome |
| Buyer learning | Actual workflow, need, capacity, systems, objections and corrections; separate inference |
| Next step | Concrete task, owner, due date/time zone and whether the prospect requested it |
| Permission / suppression | Applicable channel permission, opt-out/do-not-contact state and review; never inferred from mere contact availability |

Do not label a planned call as attempted or a draft as sent. Delivery unknown is not success and must not trigger blind resending. Keep recording/transcription off unless the authorized task and applicable consent/retention requirements support it. Save concise relevant notes rather than unnecessary personal detail.

## Funnel and learning review

Count source-backed stage transitions: researched → reviewed → authorized action attempted → conversation → buyer-confirmed need → pilot proposed → accepted/paid → delivered. Use the actual stage, not the most optimistic one. Report denominators, date windows, duplicates and missing outcomes. A small selected sample does not establish market conversion rates.

Revisit the ICP when buyers reject the premise or existing tools solve it. Record which hypothesis changed, the evidence and the next bounded test. Respect a no-contact outcome across later batches and channels. Do not generate follow-ups simply because a CRM date passed; the required authorization and current context still apply.
