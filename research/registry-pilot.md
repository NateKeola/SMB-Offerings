# Bounded public-registry pilot: what it taught us

Historical project report from November 2, 2026; consolidated November 3. The five-row pilot was not repeated or expanded for this handoff.

Source: City of Santa Monica [Active Business Licenses](https://data.santamonica.gov/dataset/active-business-licenses), official CKAN data. The prior run read five records with a `limit=5` request and recorded ODC-BY licensing and a November 2 source update. Preserve the source attribution and recheck its current terms before a new use.

**Result: zero qualified prototype opportunities.** No business names, addresses, contact details or raw records are reproduced here.

| Anonymized result | Count | Why it did not qualify |
|---|---:|---|
| Property-holding records | 2 | Registration did not establish the intended customer-facing workflow |
| Practice with unverified first-party site | 1 | Website/business association was not established |
| Studio with unresolved identity | 1 | Ambiguous identity prevented a valid website assessment |
| Landscape firm with verified website | 1 | No demonstrated task failure or source-backed pain |

This tiny, unfiltered sample does not establish that the city lacks opportunities or that official datasets are poor sources. It demonstrates why registrations must pass identity, operation, fit and task-evidence checks before becoming leads.

## Proposed next sample

Choose customer-facing business-type and city filters aligned with an explicit ICP. Review a small permitted subset, resolve DBA/branch/site matches, and use rendered public pages to test the actual task. Count verified identities, accessible websites, assessable tasks, observed friction and qualified opportunities separately. Keep ambiguous and no-issue outcomes in the denominator.

Do not label a blank website field “no website.” Do not pitch a new site until a careful identity/presence review supports the conclusion. Do not expand or download the registry merely because it is public; first explain the exact item and reason and establish permitted use.

The broader [registry source review](https://github.com/NateKeola/Dogfood/blob/c33eb2122fafe787ed65619bf4dcde2bc6fa7b7c/projects/outreach-engine/docs/REGISTRIES.md) also covers Los Angeles City/County and field/licensing caveats. It is discovery research, not a database of verified buyers.
