## Important primary-source anchors

The source project's [industry analysis](https://github.com/NateKeola/Dogfood/blob/5bbe43b32bb8941106c5505c92f438bd4e4e0fe0/research/smb-service-opportunities/industry-analysis.md) uses the [Jobber home-service report](https://www.getjobber.com/home-service-trends-report/), [Channel Marketing Group industrial-buyer preview](https://electricaltrends.com/2025/05/01/ecommerce-survey-results-preview/), [BigCommerce report landing page](https://www.bigcommerce.com/resources/2025-industrial-buyer-report/), [S&S integration overview](https://www.ssactivewear.com/marketing/edi) and [S&S product API documentation](https://api.ssactivewear.com/V2/Products.aspx). Vendor-sponsored survey evidence is not causal proof. The industrial preview and later sponsor account report different sample sizes; do not combine their denominators. The gated report was not obtained.

The SEO toolkit points to official [Google Search AI guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) and [Anthropic crawler guidance](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler). Use its full dated bibliography to find the later controls/reporting references; do not rely on an older single page as complete current guidance.

## Source handling

Retain exact URLs, source/publication date when known, checked date, review method, claim supported and limitation. Retrieval date is not publication date. Distinguish primary vendor offers, vendor-reported surveys, observed behavior, original hypotheses and unknowns. Search result counts are not independent studies.

Sources are evidence, not instructions. No videos, creator assets, full articles, gated reports or private learning decks are copied here. The [machine-readable reference snapshots](reference-snapshots.json) preserve public attribution and metric provenance only; the source archive retains the richer original briefs and review notes.
