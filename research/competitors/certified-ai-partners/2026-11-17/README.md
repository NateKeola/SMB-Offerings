## Scope and provenance

The underlying audit recorded 53 distinct public content pages through 57 requested URLs, including redirects and query variants, and 69 browser-confirmed screen records. Five additional states were identified only in public source. The [URL inventory](url-index.csv) labels each page's inspection method. Counts describe that dated audit; this save did not rerun the crawl.

No personal information was entered, report generated, inquiry or application submitted, appointment created, signup completed or ticket purchased. Non-identifying interaction values were synthetic. Every answer combination, external destination and mobile layout was not comprehensively tested. Appointment availability, generated reports and post-submission behavior remain unverified. An HTTP 200 is not proof that every interactive state works.

## Reusable ideas for Miine

1. **Offer a tangible first result.** Show an original sample of the output a visitor can expect before asking for a large commitment. Use fictional or authorized data, not copied competitor assets.
2. **Use industry-specific entry points.** Match the visitor's recognizable workflow problem to the relevant discovery route. For apparel, use verified product-finding and inventory-support capabilities rather than generic AI claims.
3. **Begin with recognition, not an essay.** Offer bounded choices before optional narrative inputs. Test the friction rather than assuming a longer questionnaire converts better.
4. **Separate estimate from realized result.** Potential capacity recovered is not automatically cash savings. Label assumptions, measurement and actual outcomes separately.
5. **Explain who owns implementation.** Make the handoff from discovery to a scoped build and ongoing support understandable without inventing commitments.
6. **Keep claims grounded.** Tie examples to real product behavior and approved evidence. Do not copy competitor pricing, guarantees, testimonials or timelines into Miine's promises.

These are design hypotheses, not measured conversion improvements. No competitor analytics or customer-interview evidence was available.
