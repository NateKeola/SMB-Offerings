## Evidence classes

**Observed public facts:** published offer prices/scope, documented integration surfaces, official access/search guidance. **Vendor-reported evidence:** survey results and claims, subject to methodology and commercial interests. **Original hypotheses:** chosen ICP, scope/effort ranges, prices to test, support/CAC/churn and margins. **Unknowns:** willingness to pay, local prevalence, conversion uplift, recurring customer value, actual API spending and Reddit customer-pain evidence.

No internal account data or private research artifacts are included. The separate API-spend work should consume actual costs privately and keep public fixtures distinct from verified bills.
