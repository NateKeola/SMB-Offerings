## Decision

Alternative considered: generic AI receptionist sold as missed-revenue recovery. Rejected as the default because coverage already exists in the industry, proof of funeral-specific delivery is absent, and emotional, legal and dispatch consequences require stronger controls.

This recommendation is a research hypothesis, not user selection of a market, a vendor purchase, an outbound campaign or approval to deploy. The repository's existing restrictions remain unchanged.
