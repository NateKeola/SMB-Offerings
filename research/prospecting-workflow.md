# Evolving prospecting workflow

Recorded and expanded 2026-11-07. **Proposed framework**, grounded in the user's reported pathway of finding businesses in search, inspecting their presence and calling three roofers. Later context points to a private worksheet; reconcile its examples privately. Exact search context, sequence and call-to-business mapping need verification. This is not a description of a working agent or a completed audit.

Use the [agency/ICP working record](../strategy/agency-direction-and-icp.md), [qualification gates](../strategy/opportunity-qualification.md) and [opportunity template](../templates/opportunity-record.md). Extend the process as the user supplies details; do not replace their pathway with an invented funnel.

## 1. Capture the actual pathway

Record the next real example step by step in an approved private workspace:

| Stage | Capture | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Search | Query, search surface, location context, timestamp, ad/map/organic distinction and displayed position | Position is context for this search, not a universal ranking or quality score |
| Resolve identity | Business, branch, official site and the reason the match is credible | A registry row or similar name alone does not confirm identity |
| Inspect site | Service/customer task, tested route, expected next step, observed result and control | Record no issue, unknown and not assessable alongside friction |
| Inspect response channels | Public phone, form, chat, text or booking options and visible stated hours | A widget's absence does not prove a channel is absent or nonfunctional |
| Call or submit, if separately authorized | Human/agent/voicemail outcome, time to useful response, questions, next step and handoff | Do not place calls, send messages, submit forms or create bookings from this research template |
| Interpret | Separate observed behavior, user's interpretation, hypothesis and missing information | No invented lost leads, revenue, conversion rate or technical provider |
| Qualify | Buyer-confirmed need, capacity, fit, evidence confidence, data access and scope | A feature gap alone is not a qualified opportunity |
| Choose next action | Hold, disqualify, gather one missing fact or prepare a bounded demonstration | An approved preparation record is not approval to contact or deploy |

Preserve original observations and dates when an interpretation changes. Capture the user's actual navigation order and reasons for moving to the next business; the assistant should propose additions explicitly rather than describe them as steps the user already took.

## 2. Website and journey checks

A check must describe a real user task and its result. Use these outcome labels: **observed working**, **observed friction**, **not observed in this check**, **unknown**, **not assessable**, **not applicable**.

- **Service clarity:** Can a customer establish the service, location coverage, hours and next step?
- **Mobile route:** Are the relevant page, navigation, call link and inquiry controls usable in the tested viewport?
- **Inquiry path:** Is the expected form or booking route visible and understandable? Read-only inspection does not establish submission, receipt or follow-up.
- **Phone/text/chat:** What channels are visibly offered? Which were actually tested by an authorized person? Distinguish human, automated, ambiguous and no answer.
- **Handoff:** Is the promised next step clear? Was receipt or a callback actually confirmed, or only described?
- **Catalog task, where relevant:** Can a known item be found by its exact name/code, then by a realistic description or attribute? Do not infer the search architecture from one result.
- **Business information:** Check public profile/site consistency with sources; resolve branch differences rather than treating them as errors.
- **Reviews and trust:** Record counts, recency and visible patterns as context. Do not assume review count explains rank or demand.
- **Search foundations:** Identify specific public page/indexability/content issues using the [existing SEO playbook](../seo/search-and-ai-visibility.md). Analytics, traffic and revenue remain unknown without a permitted source.

For every potential gap, ask: **What customer task does it affect, how often, how severely, and does the owner recognize it?** Keep a successful control and repeated observation before presenting a reproducible issue. An inaccessible site or blocked tool fetch is not negative evidence about the business.

## 3. Comparable customer categories

These are **research comparisons**, not selected ICPs or claims that all firms in a category share a problem.

| Category | Comparable task worth testing | Important difference |
|---|---|---|
| Roofing and exterior contractors | Quote intake, inspection scheduling and follow-up | Project-specific scope and human-approved pricing; often less immediate than emergency repair |
| HVAC and plumbing | Missed/after-hours inquiry routing and scheduling | Urgency, dispatch capacity and safe escalation; do not automate emergency triage |
| Electrical services | Availability, service-area checks and appointment requests | Safety-sensitive situations and licensing/task boundaries |
| Landscaping and irrigation | Service qualification, estimates and recurring-work inquiries | Seasonal demand, property-specific scope and route capacity |
| Cleaning and other scheduled local services | Scope clarification, scheduling and recurring inquiries | Existing booking tools may already solve the task |
| Automotive repair | Appointment requests and status routing | Diagnosis, parts availability and shop-system integration require careful scope |
| Wholesale/catalog businesses | Product discovery and order preparation | A separate buyer journey with catalog, stock, variant and pricing dependencies |

Compare workflows, buyer access and delivery constraints before assuming that an industry label predicts fit. A large firm with a polished site may still have a painful internal task; a low-ranking firm with no visible agent may have no useful need.

## 4. Screening criteria

Apply the existing hard qualification gates first. Then keep a short evidence-backed assessment of:

- Clear customer task and repeated, material friction.
- Owner/team recognition of the problem and a reachable decision role.
- Capacity and operational reason to benefit from the improvement.
- Adequacy of existing tools and a credible advantage over configuring them.
- Permitted data, system access, integration effort and ongoing support burden.
- Baseline, measurable acceptance criteria and human fallback.
- Willingness to discuss a bounded pilot and pay for a verified outcome.
- Legal, privacy, safety and platform constraints that could change scope.

**Hold or disqualify** when identity is unresolved, access is blocked, the task works adequately, demand/capacity is absent, the owner does not recognize the problem, evidence is too weak, or delivery cannot be scoped responsibly.

Search position, review count, site age and presence/absence of an agent may help select questions. They are not standalone approval criteria, evidence of pain or a composite score that overrides a hold.

## 5. Proposed agent harness

This describes requirements for a later build. **No harness is implemented or activated by this document.**

1. **Task specification:** Receive the chosen market, geography, actual user pathway, task to inspect, allowed sources, exclusions, batch/query caps and budget. Leave unspecified criteria open.
2. **Read-only collection:** Resolve identity, inspect public pages and capture source/date/task evidence. Respect site rules, access restrictions and usage limits.
3. **Observation ledger:** Store what was attempted and observed with the outcome labels above. Preserve human, agent and synthetic origins separately.
4. **Hypothesis layer:** Suggest why a failure might matter and what question would distinguish explanations. Never transform a suggestion into a verified observation.
5. **Human review:** Require review of identity, evidence, fit, privacy and the proposed next step. Review binds to the current record, not an older version.
6. **Authorized action boundary:** Calls, submissions, messages, bookings, account access, paid services and deployments require their own applicable permission. This documentation grants none.
7. **Learning update:** Record what the user corrected, what the buyer confirmed, which experiment occurred and the measured outcome. Update the ICP without erasing contrary evidence.

Keep business/contact records, screenshots with personal data, call material and private system details out of this public repository. Do not record or transcribe calls merely because a provider supports it; establish the applicable consent and retention requirements first.

### Proposed acceptance checks

- Replaying the same permitted task produces an auditable observation trail, not a fabricated score.
- An unavailable/blocked source remains unknown or not assessable.
- No-agent and low-search-position observations never become an automatic sales diagnosis.
- Existing tools and no-issue outcomes can disqualify a candidate.
- A synthetic example cannot qualify a real business.
- A changed source or user correction invalidates the relevant prior review.
- The harness performs no external action or paid operation without the required permission.
- The user can see what changed in the ICP and which evidence supports the change.

## 7. Compare strong and weak journeys

The user wants to study effective same-industry websites and blogs, then turn useful patterns into improvements. A high search position, paid ad or polished site is a comparison candidate, not proof of conversion performance.

1. Choose the same customer task, service/product type and relevant geography. Record why examples are comparable and what differs.
2. Record the query and distinguish ads, Maps and organic results; inspect a bounded set of pages, not an invented exhaustive index.
3. Map homepage → relevant service/product page → supporting information → contact/quote route. Include mobile behavior, visible trust evidence, CTA wording, fields and next-step expectations.
4. For a useful blog/resource, record its URL, topic, user question, recency, original observations and links into a genuine service/product path. Volume, word count and keywords alone do not establish usefulness or ranking causation.
5. Write the transferable pattern in original words: for example, clear service eligibility before an inquiry, or an appropriate RFQ route from a product page. Record the task benefit and a test that could confirm it.
6. Distinguish structural inspiration from permission to reproduce text, photography, code, branding, testimonials or full pages. Build original assets/copy or use separately authorized material; do not create misleading local pages or false claims.

Keep a private comparison matrix with tested page, task, working pattern, friction, visibility context, evidence date and untested conversion steps. A public pattern library should contain reviewed original summaries and permitted public sources, not prospect dossiers. It should improve the [ICP cards](../strategy/icp-discovery-cards.md), not replace buyer discovery.

For source ordering, storage and automation acceptance, use the [research harness contract](../architecture/prospect-research-harness.md). For actual calls and follow-up outcomes, use the [private activity ledger](../templates/research-and-contact-ledger.md), not the public research notes.
