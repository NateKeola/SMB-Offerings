# Astra build prompt: Contractor License Lead Audit

## Mission
Build an internal, evidence-backed web app that turns official contractor licensing records into a prioritized, human-reviewed business-development queue. Start in Los Angeles, Ventura, and Santa Barbara counties, expand across California through configuration, and prepare adapters for Washington, Oregon, and Arizona. The desired workflow is license discovery → business/entity resolution → legally permitted business-profile matching → website and profile audit → human review → a genuinely usable calling queue. Build an operational application, not merely a landing page or mock dashboard. Do not send messages, place calls, create accounts, buy data, or enable paid services automatically.

## Non-negotiable source and Google boundaries
Implement a source-rights registry before enrichment. Each source must specify owner, URL, access method, permitted purpose, allowed fields, retention, redistribution/export rights, review date, approval evidence, and enabled state. Public availability does not establish unrestricted commercial reuse. Unknown permissions fail closed. Keep collection, display, scoring, retention, and export permissions separate.

Google Business Profile APIs manage authorized merchant accounts; they are not an unrestricted prospecting directory. The GoogleLocations endpoint requires an existing merchant relationship and is limited to creating/claiming locations. Never use it for cold-prospect discovery. Places API and GBP APIs are different integrations.

Google Maps Platform terms restrict scraping/extraction, caching, creation of content derived from Maps content, and use in listings/directory services or to create or augment an advertising product. Therefore Google-based prospect matching/auditing must be disabled until a documented review confirms that this exact use case and its data flows are permitted, including any necessary written rights. Do not imply an API key, transient fetching, place-ID storage, or manual transcription solves the use-case restriction. Never implement a scraper or buy scraped Google data as a workaround.

If a permitted Google mode is established, enforce the approved field-level retention rules and current display/attribution requirements. Place IDs have a storage exception; do not generalize it to business names, phones, addresses, reviews, ratings, photos, screenshots, embeddings, or derivative scores. Restrict logs, analytics, caches, exports, model inputs, and backups too. Do not assume a blanket 30-day caching allowance. Keep first-party/source-licensed fields physically and logically distinct. Do not let copying or re-keying a Google value relabel it as independent evidence. The default MVP remains useful without Google: authoritative licenses, independently sourced business contacts, first-party website audits, and manual review. A separate future connected-customer module may audit a customer's own authorized GBP with the applicable permissions.

## Official-source adapters
California: use the CSLB Public Data Portal's statewide master download or county/classification exports, not browser scraping of every license. The master covers renewed and expired-but-renewable licenses and omits cancelled, revoked, and expired nonrenewable licenses. Do not call every imported record active or interpret absence from a later file as proof of revocation. Available master fields include license number, business name/address/phone, status, dates, and classifications; CSLB does not supply email addresses. Read current file schema and snapshot date rather than assuming a format or inventing an API. Prefer the minimum master fields; personnel, insurance-policy identifiers, and worker-compensation details are unnecessary for marketing and excluded by default. Provide the official current-status check link and an explicit recheck workflow before qualification.

Initial counties are configurable FIPS/county records: Los Angeles 06037, Ventura 06111, Santa Barbara 06083. Distinguish mailing location, operating location, and claimed service area. Do not treat ZIP or license mailing address as definitive territory coverage. Show inferred county and its method/confidence. Enable statewide California with the same schema, not a rewrite.

Future adapters remain disabled until source and rights review: Washington L&I's official general contractor dataset m8qx-ubtq; Oregon CCB Active Licenses g77e-6bhs; Arizona ROC Posting List. Washington's contractor license number is the source key and one UBI can have several registrations. Review Washington's restriction on lists of individuals requested for commercial purposes, especially sole proprietors; do not equate an open-data download with clearance. Oregon provides a self-service active-license/mailing dataset. Arizona's posting-list page was search-discovered but direct retrieval returned 403 during this research; verify current download schema, terms, commercial-use conditions, and lawful access before activation. Do not bypass access restrictions. State taxonomies differ, so map them to internal trades without pretending classifications are identical.

## Data model and ingestion
Use PostgreSQL, durable background jobs, object storage only for permitted artifacts, and a typed API/frontend; adapt to the existing repository stack. Keep businesses, legal entities, licenses, locations, trade classifications, contacts, sources, ingestion runs, evidence, match candidates, reviewer decisions, audit findings, suppression records, and outreach tasks as separate entities. A business can have multiple licenses and locations; one license can cover multiple classifications. Enforce (jurisdiction, issuing agency, license number) uniqueness, not company-name uniqueness.

Store original and normalized values, source timestamps, retrieved timestamps, schema/parser version, provenance, and allowed retention/export policy per field. Use idempotent imports, checksums, row-level validation, quarantine for malformed rows, restartable jobs, and snapshot diffs. Never silently overwrite conflicting facts. Missing data stays unknown. Detect stale snapshots and schema changes, pause affected jobs, and show actionable errors. Provide an import preview with accepted/rejected counts and no writes until approved. Synthetic fixtures must be visibly labeled and never mixed with live records.

## Business resolution and matching
Generate candidates using normalized legal/DBA names, independently sourced business phone, domain, address, city, and geography. Maintain reversible aliases and versioned normalization. Do not merge solely on a similar name, shared office, shared tracking phone, common qualifier, franchise brand, or near-identical street address. Support service-area businesses without public storefront addresses, relocated companies, subsidiaries, and multi-location organizations. Preserve multiple plausible candidates and rejection reasons.

Show interpretable feature contributions, contradictory evidence, candidate runner-up margin, and separate identity confidence from sales attractiveness. Exact license number on a business's own website plus compatible name/geography is strong evidence, but corroborate it and detect copied/obsolete content. High-confidence automatic suggestions are not automatic irreversible merges. Calibrate thresholds against a labeled pilot set; do not invent probabilistic certainty. Human choices: confirm, reject, needs research, duplicate, wrong company, stale, out of scope. Record who, when, why, and evidence; allow undo/split.

A missing search result means 'not found in this search on this date,' never 'no Google profile exists.' API errors, hidden service-area addresses, query limits, and exhausted budgets must never become negative business facts. Claim/verification status is unknown unless an authorized source establishes it. Do not promise complete Google coverage or ranking measurements.

## Audit and score
Audit independently located first-party business websites only with permitted access, conservative concurrency, and no login/captcha bypass. Check observable contactability: HTTPS behavior, reachable pages, business contact visibility, working click-to-call links, mobile layout heuristics, visible service/territory descriptions, lead-form presence (never submit), and observable broken paths. Label heuristic and automated tests as such. 'No website located' differs from 'no website exists.' Never infer revenue, missed calls, owner identity, customer dissatisfaction, or search ranking from weak proxies.

Each finding must have a specific observation, source URL, observed time, confidence, evidence rights, recheck date, and an honest business implication. Save excerpts/screenshots only when their rights permit retention; never retain unauthorized Google evidence. Use permitted non-Google evidence for durable scores. Separate identity confidence, evidence freshness, contact readiness, opportunity, and compliance state. Offer transparent editable scoring weights; use no unsupported revenue estimates. Unknown signals do not equal poor performance. An active license or low review count alone is not an opportunity claim.

## Calling queue and human review
The main views are Import/Source Health, Territory & Trade Explorer, Candidate Match Review, Business Audit, Calling Queue, Suppressions, and Settings/Cost. Detail pages show provenance beside each fact, conflicts, linked licenses/locations, and change history. Prioritization should surface a concrete, verifiable reason for a useful conversation.

'Call ready' requires verified business identity, an eligible recent license-status check under configured policy, an independently sourced business number with provenance and verification date, relevant territory/trade, a fresh supported opportunity, completed human review, and clearance of applicable suppression/compliance checks. A syntactically valid number is not proven reachable; distinguish format-valid, source-confirmed, independently validated, and manually reached. Do not place validation calls. Cellular/personal/shared/unknown numbers require appropriate additional review; public listing is not consent.

Apply seller-specific do-not-contact records across every merged alias/number, deduplicate at business and contact levels, and preserve opt-outs across reimports. Show recipient-local permitted calling windows, configurable cooldowns, existing-customer/existing-conversation context, and last disposition. Federal/state telemarketing, TCPA, privacy, recording, DNC, and business-contact applicability require reviewed policy, not a blanket B2B exemption. Washington rollout needs separate commercial-use review. No autonomous calls, texts, emails, autodialing, recording, or marketing consent assumptions. A phone link is user-triggered. Draft a short truthful opening supported by the evidence; never say the caller represents Google or guarantees rankings.

Export only source-authorized fields from reviewed records: internal business ID, business name, jurisdiction/license, trade, territory, independently sourced business phone/website, approved finding, evidence URL/date, review status, next action, and suppression/compliance status. Block restricted content, home-address leakage, policy/financial identifiers, and unneeded personal data. Protect CSV exports against formula injection and keep an export audit log. A callable export is preparation for human action, not permission to contact anyone.

## Scale, security, and operations
Use indexed server-side filtering/pagination, cursor-based ingestion, queue partitions by source/state, bounded concurrency, exponential backoff with jitter, dead-letter queues, deduplicated job keys, and incremental rechecks. Avoid pairwise comparisons across all businesses: block candidates by compatible geography, identifiers, name tokens, and domain. Separate deterministic parsing/matching from optional model-assisted review; models must never invent contacts or overwrite verified facts.

Before enrichment, show an estimated request count and cost range, source quotas, daily hard budget, and pause control. Use field masks/minimal payloads where applicable and only after a source passes the use-case gate. Cache solely when permitted, not merely to reduce cost. Add per-source kill switches, request accounting, permission-expiry checks, retention deletion jobs, and tests proving logs/backups do not leak restricted payloads. Keep API credentials server-side and support least-privilege roles, encryption, audit logs, abuse protection, access revocation, retention controls, and deletion/suppression requests.

## Delivery and acceptance
Deliver migrations, working UI/API/jobs, source adapters, documented configuration, clearly labeled synthetic seed data, unit/integration/end-to-end tests, threat/rights notes, runbook, and a phased rollout plan. External integrations requiring credentials or approval must show 'not configured' or 'blocked pending rights review,' never fabricated success.

Pilot: ingest an approved official California snapshot for the three counties, report coverage and exclusions, review a labeled sample across trades and ambiguous identities, and present measured precision/error categories and cost per reviewed record. Proposed release targets, to validate rather than assert achieved: at least 99% precision for the high-confidence suggested-match bucket on an adequately sized adjudicated sample; zero silent merges; 100% exported fields have allowed provenance; zero suppressed contacts in callable exports; zero Google-derived restricted payloads in persistent storage. Track recall/unknowns separately and report sample size and uncertainty. Test duplicates, stale licenses, out-of-county mailing addresses, service-area businesses, multiple branches, unavailable sources, empty fields, permission changes, 429s, quota exhaustion, and job restarts. Benchmark the full licensed population size actually observed plus a clearly labeled scale fixture, rather than inventing record counts.

Phase 1: rights-aware California ingestion, entity resolution, website audit, human review, safe export. Phase 2: validated statewide expansion and better incremental updates. Phase 3: permitted Google/connected-customer capabilities after rights verification. Phase 4: separately approved WA/OR/AZ adapters. Do not let an unavailable Google integration block useful, compliant Phase 1 work.
