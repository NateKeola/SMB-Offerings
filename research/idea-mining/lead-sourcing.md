# Permitted prospect sourcing and qualification

## Research design

Start with legitimate businesses, then verify specific digital gaps. An unattractive site does not establish buying intent.

Use county × city × trade batches. Illustrative markets are Los Angeles, Ventura and Santa Barbara counties; city-level research reduces countywide sampling bias. Suggested initial trades: plumbing C-36, HVAC C-20, electrical C-10, roofing C-39, landscaping C-27, painting C-33 and tree/palm C-49. Prioritization is a hypothesis, not demand evidence.

CSLB county/classification records offer business, phone, license/status and classifications, not email. Registration/licensing is not proof of current activity or capacity. Recheck applicable status. No dataset was downloaded or actual lead list created in this pass.

## Reusable sourcing prompt

Find up to [COUNT, e.g. 30] independently operated [TRADES] businesses across [COUNTIES/CITIES]. Aim for a balanced sample, but do not pad it. Start with permitted official records, business-owned sites, trade/chamber directories and appropriately licensed sources. Check reuse rights before exporting or persisting data.

Confirm identity, canonical domain, actual services, geographic fit, current operation and required license. Deduplicate by license, domain, business identity and relevant phone/location; distinguish related organizations and branches.

Classify website status as present/usable, social-only, parked/broken, or no independent website found after specified checks. A missing directory link is not proof there is no website. Require at least two concrete, fixable gaps for this proposed qualification workflow: mobile obstruction, broken navigation/contact destination, unclear services/service area, or useful missing service information. Inspect forms without submission.

Assess search only with recorded query, location, date, result type and inspected depth. Say not observed in the sampled results, not does not rank. Treat missing review evidence as unknown, not zero. Compare only genuinely similar local businesses. If using a review-gap screen, label thresholds such as fewer than 20 reviews and below one quarter of five comparable peers' median as adjustable heuristics, not standards or evidence of dissatisfaction.

Separate digital opportunity from likely buyer. Published expansion, new services or stated availability may be timing evidence; do not invent budget, revenue, desperation or spare capacity. Find only public business contact routes, not fabricated owners/emails or private contact data.

Return identity, city/county, category, verified website/status, source links/date, operating/license evidence, concrete gaps, permitted review observations, comparable-business evidence, proposed service, factual personalization angle, confidence, unknowns and next validation. Keep real records in an approved private workspace, never these public repositories.

Do not contact anyone, submit forms, create accounts, buy data, circumvent restrictions or collect more than permitted. Exclude closed entities, misleading lead-generation listings and unresolved identity matches. Return a separate verification queue and honest shortfall explanation.

## Qualification and priority

Prefer the shared 0–10 opportunity framework in the SEO prompt rather than accumulating incompatible composite scores. Require verified identity, geography and operation plus concrete fixable evidence. The two-gap rule is this batch's proposed research filter; a severe single issue can merit manual review rather than automatic rejection.

Ready for research is not ready for outreach. Human review must verify the specific finding, contact route, freshness and communication authorization. No discovery source creates sending permission.
