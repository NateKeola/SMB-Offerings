## Ulysses: local-service audit and rebuild

Source: https://www.instagram.com/reels/DeGOqPbya4i/
Public author: @ulysses.happymouths.ai. Duration approximately 2:07. Advice sections were checked through visible subtitles across playback; audio was not independently transcribed.

The source presents a roofing-business audit/rebuild pitch. Observed topics: branding, a reportedly broken site, interactive lead capture, title/H1 alignment with business category, consistent business information, individual service pages, map embed, schema, sitemap/robots/llms files, local rank heatmap, personalized requests to prior customers, directory consistency, service-area address handling, relevant local links, Apple Maps presence, hours, business-name consistency and About content. Review-response advice is a paraphrase inferred from the ending, not a verified literal quotation. The precise title-tag wording and one sentence preceding phone consistency were not verified.

Its review/customer counts and unsourced distrust statistic are unverified source claims, not current facts or evidence of a sale. They are not used to justify this service.

Original adaptation: diagnose task friction and identity consistency first; build only useful customer journeys; use neutral, permitted review requests and authorized assets. Do not turn every checklist item into a universal ranking requirement.

Qualifications:
- Google says llms.txt is not needed for Search and does not help or harm rankings: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide
- Hide an address when customers are not served there, not simply because it is residential: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
- Map embeds, exact-match headings, schema and interactive forms do not guarantee ranking gains. Local factors: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091
- Sponsored links require appropriate qualification: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links

## Yates: website compliance starter checklist

Public author: @yatesvids. Duration approximately 31.8 seconds. Opening/ending and all 19 visible checklist items were reviewed; audio was not independently transcribed.

Original adaptation: use the [compliance research prompt](compliance-research.md) to determine applicability, ownership and evidence before implementation. Do not create generic legal policies that describe nonexistent practices.

## Zach P: on-page SEO topics

Source: https://www.instagram.com/reels/DcUCK5LiGiU/
Public author: @zach_weup, not the similarly named @zackkirk_. Duration approximately 41.6 seconds. Visible checklist/captions reviewed throughout; audio not independently transcribed. No downloaded media was used.

Observed topics: Search Console indexing; breadcrumbs; canonicals; Core Web Vitals; orphan pages; schema; headings; original/duplicate content; backlinks; descriptive URLs; broken links/404s; crawlable JavaScript; keyword overlap; mobile; author bio/E-E-A-T; titles; unique meta descriptions; thin/overlapping pages; images/alt text; search intent; keyword demand/competition; internal links.

The title advice showed 50–60 with no verified unit. The thin/overlapping-pages action verb was ambiguous; this report records only the topic and does not silently change it into a claimed instruction to merge pages. The promotional value headline is not evidence of economic value.

Qualifications:
- No fixed 50–60-character title limit: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link
- Not every excluded URL or 404 requires repair: https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7440203
- Canonicals are signals, not guaranteed directives: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls
- Duplication is not automatically spam: https://developers.google.com/search/help/crawling-index-faq
- E-E-A-T is not a single ranking factor; a bio alone is insufficient: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- Good page experience and schema do not guarantee ranking/rich results: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/page-experience and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies
- Avoid manufactured ranking links: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies

Original adaptation: verify a bounded sample and prioritize user/search-intent problems using [the audit framework](seo-audit.md), keeping public evidence separate from owner-only analytics.
