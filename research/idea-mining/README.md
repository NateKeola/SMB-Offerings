# SMB idea mining and reusable research prompts

Prepared 2026-11-06. This is an original, public-safe research and service-design package, not a customer list, operating product, legal opinion or record of private conversations.

## Purpose and priorities

Investigate useful improvements for established service businesses: evidence-backed website/local-search diagnosis, permitted lead sourcing, rights-aware redesign, scoped technical compliance support, and review/after-hours workflows. Southern California counties are illustrative research markets, not evidence of demand or a commitment to a single vertical.

Start with a real customer or staff task, document the existing pattern, propose a specific improvement, and identify the next evidence needed. Keep discovery, validation, implementation, outreach and paid delivery separate. A popular tutorial, visually polished demo or inexpensive model does not prove willingness to pay.

All six are proposed service directions. No new customer audit, lead list, prototype, live integration or outcome was produced by this documentation pass.

## Next bounded research pass

Select one service category and three city-level markets. Review five candidates per market, with an honest shortfall if evidence is weak. Use only permitted public sources; no purchased datasets or active outreach is implied. Evaluate practical problems and false positives before expanding. Choose a pilot only after buyer confirmation, explicit scope, required access, cost cap and acceptance tests.

Do not infer authorization to contact businesses, collect customer data, install software, activate providers, purchase, publish a client site or create a recurring schedule from a reusable prompt.
