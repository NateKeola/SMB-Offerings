# Website compliance research and technical support

This is a reusable research specification, not legal advice, a client-specific legal conclusion or a promise of comprehensive compliance.

## Reusable research prompt

Research a practical website compliance-support service for SMBs, prioritizing the United States and California, with food-seller and children's-service modules. State research date and verify current laws, regulations, effective/compliance dates, regulator guidance and material litigation before conclusions.

### Scope and responsibility

Distinguish binding legal requirements, technical standards, contractual requirements, best practices and unresolved questions. Explain what an agency can inspect/implement/document, what the client must operate, and what qualified counsel or a specialist must decide. Avoid unauthorized legal practice or marketing technical checks as legal certification.

### Intake and applicability

Ask only necessary facts: entity and operating/customer jurisdictions; physical premises and government relationships; products/services/audiences and actual knowledge of minors; applicable revenue/data thresholds; sites/apps/languages/CMS/checkout/booking/documents/vendors; data categories, sources, uses, recipients, retention and sensitive data; advertising, analytics, replay, chat and cookies; newsletter/SMS/subscriptions/promotions/reviews/refunds/shipping/payments; existing policies/contracts/consent records/reports/complaints and responsible owners.

Do not assume children's products make a service child-directed, small size exempts every law, or a California connection triggers every requirement.

### General SMB analysis

Cover truthful claims, endorsements/reviews, fees/pricing, shipping/refunds and renewal flows. Analyze CalOPPA separately from CCPA/CPRA; verify current adjusted thresholds, definitions, exceptions and service-provider obligations. Assess notices, actual processing, requests, retention/deletion, vendors, security, sale/share opt-outs and recognized preference signals where applicable.

Consider other state privacy/consumer-health laws only where activities create exposure. GDPR/UK and international cookie/accessibility rules are conditional expansions. Assess email/SMS consent and unsubscribe operation. Distinguish unsettled tracking/session-replay litigation from settled duties. Treat PCI DSS as a payment-industry standard/contractual issue, not a universal statute.

Check current litigation before calling any federal click-to-cancel rule operative; assess ROSCA and applicable state automatic-renewal laws separately. Never generate final legal terms from guessed business practices.

### Accessibility module

Separate ADA Title III private businesses, Title II public entities and Section 508. Explain online-only/jurisdictional uncertainty without outcome guarantees. A scoped WCAG 2.2 AA technical benchmark is not automatically the applicable legal standard.

Combine automated and manual tests: keyboard, focus, screen reader, landmarks/headings, labels/errors/authentication, checkout/bookings, contrast, zoom/reflow, reduced motion, captions/alternatives, touch targets/mobile, PDFs/menus and third-party widgets. Define sample, browser/assistive-technology combinations, severity, remediation and retest. A scan, overlay or accessibility statement does not establish comprehensive compliance.

### Food module

Separate restaurants, packaged foods, manufacturers, marketplaces, cottage-food sellers, meal subscriptions, supplements, alcohol and infant/children's foods. Check ingredient/allergen requirements including sesame; distinguish packaged labels from restaurant disclosure. Verify nutrition exemptions, chain-menu criteria and covered online menus. Check substantiation for health/nutrient/organic/gluten-free claims; Proposition 65 applicability, exemptions, exposure assessment and internet-sale warnings; supplier accuracy, recalls, delivery and fees.

Do not invent ingredient/nutrition/allergen/warning information or assume physical-label rules always create identical online duties. Name client/specialist owners. Operational licensing is separate scope.

### Children module

Distinguish child-directed, mixed/general audiences, actual knowledge, adult-facing retailers, schools and teens. Verify amended COPPA rules, age thresholds, notices, parental consent/exceptions, advertising/disclosure, minimization, security, retention, parental rights and vendor obligations. Assess age-screening risks and additional data collection. School authorization is not unlimited; FERPA does not cover every children's business. Verify state minors laws, injunctions and dates. A 13+ label or age gate alone does not settle applicability. Require qualified review for consequential age/consent architecture.

### Outputs and service design

For each finding give client facts/missing facts, exact authority and official URL, jurisdiction, effective date and check date, requirement class, action/owner/priority, dependency and acceptance test.

Deliver intake/decision tree; law/control matrix; data-flow/vendor inventory; accessibility sample and test plan; prioritized remediation backlog; policy requirements and counsel-review checklist; evidence register; handoff/maintenance owners.

Propose bounded discovery, technical remediation and periodic retest packages, with food/children specialist add-ons. State page/flow/vendor limits, revision rounds, exclusions, prerequisites and change triggers. Separate counsel, specialist testing and tools. Prices must distinguish observed benchmarks from modeled effort. Use scoped technical compliance support or accessibility/privacy readiness language, never ADA certified, lawsuit-proof or 100% compliant.

End with launch plan, missing decisions, counsel escalation and decline/referral conditions. No invented citations, fines, certifications or legal conclusions.
