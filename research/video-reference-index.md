## Limits that travel with these references

- None of the tutorials was independently reproduced or its source code executed. A transcript or filmed UI does not establish a working backend, accessibility, performance or business result.
- Publication dates for the Instagram/site examples remain unknown; review dates must not be used as substitutes. Tool/version dates and current availability are not established by a recent video.
- The October and September 2026 YouTube builds are discovery examples. The 2022/2024/2025 research and sales videos are older fundamentals, not current setup or conversion guidance.
- The café source is an AI-labeled visual concept. The marina guide incentive may affect comments. The property-tour source is third-party technology commentary; footage ownership, capture, hosting and mobile performance remain unverified.
- Instagram review was muted and sampled; logged-out playback was not tested. Four YouTube entries had readable transcript coverage; the restaurant example used sampled frames, chapters/metadata and a companion article.
- Same-creator repeats and unknown origins are not independent market corroboration. Affiliate, vendor and mentorship incentives are limitations, not proof of dishonesty.
- Learn the general pattern and create original implementations with authorized assets. Do not rehost videos, screenshots, creator code or proprietary tutorial material.
