## Risks, solutions and value preserved

| Detriment / constraint | Practical solution | Customer value retained | Residual cost or tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intake may be confidential before engagement | Minimal callback fields, restricted queue, staged information requests | Faster acknowledgement and context without demanding the whole story | Counsel-approved script, vendor review and security still required |
| Conflict exposure from detailed narratives | Ask only approved party identifiers first; human clearance | Less wasted staff time and a clearer next step | Some callers must wait; a staffed reviewer is needed |
| Portal would create a new sensitive-data store | Use existing CRM permissions and workflow first | One useful queue and audit history | Existing license/API costs; native feature constraints |
| Recording/transcription consent is complex | Pre-processing consent design and accessible human alternative | Inbound availability with user choice | Vendor capability verification, fallback coverage and residual legal review |
| AI may give advice or invent context | Bounded administrative responses, source-linked fields and human correction | Accurate callback context and less repetitive data entry | QA time and restricted automation |
| Existing products already offer the features | Sell configuration, integration repair, callback ownership and measurement | Reliable real-world execution for firms without implementation capacity | Lower software moat; service labor must be profitable |
| Human follow-through is the bottleneck | Named callback owner, due times and overdue escalation | More completed conversations, not only faster bot replies | Firm capacity and labor cost; no guarantee of more retained matters |
| Synthetic prospecting could waste firm time | Authorized mystery shop or permitted retrospective audit | Reproducible response-time evidence | Recruitment/consent effort and measurement bias must be acknowledged |
| High support/escalation erodes margin | One CRM/practice/inbox, capped scope, clear change fees and provider pass-through | Predictable affordable service | Some firms will not be economic fits; custom work priced separately |
| Sensitive documents expand exposure | Delay uploads; later quarantine and controlled requests | Supplementary context when actually needed | Extra storage, malware, access, retention and review costs |
