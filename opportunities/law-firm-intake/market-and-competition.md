# Demand, alternatives and target customers

Checked 2026-11-03. Findings below are source-reported unless labeled hypothesis. Full provenance and retrieval limitations are in the [source register](sources.md).

## How big is the problem?

The available evidence justifies investigating responsiveness. It does not establish a national dollar loss, an addressable customer count or the proportion of missed inquiries that would have become profitable matters.

| Study | Method and sample | Reported result | Interpretation and limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Clio 2024 Legal Trends Report](https://www.clio.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2024-Legal-Trends-Report-Full-Publication.pdf) | Clio commissioned Lux; 500 US firms; June 20–July 5, 2024; practice-specific questions through phone, email, forms and chat. Report describes random sampling and includes Clio/non-Clio firms. | 40% answered initial calls; 52% answered or called back; 48% unreachable by phone; 33% replied to email. Among email responders, 84% replied within eight hours and all within two business days. | Measures sampled inquiries, not all incoming calls, lost clients or revenue. Do not add phone/email failures: people may use both. Exact time-of-day and sampling-frame detail are insufficient to infer an after-hours rate or California/practice-specific prevalence. Vendor-funded, third-party fieldwork; no raw-data audit here. |
| [Hennessey Digital 2025 study](https://hennessey.com/2025-lead-form-response-time-study/) | 1,333 US firms selected from DMA Report, Law Firm A-List and a curated list; Q1 2025; fictional website-form inquiries at 10 a.m.–noon local time; first personal outreach tracked for seven days. Includes PI, criminal defense and other practices; earlier firms retained for trend work. | 74% made contact within seven days; 26% did not; 25% responded in under five minutes. | Form leads and personal follow-up, not inbound phone answering or email auto-replies. Nonprobability/curated sample; legal-marketing vendor incentive; no national weighting. A seven-day cutoff does not mean nobody ever replied. |

These studies converge on incomplete follow-through while describing different performance levels. Hennessey's improvement trend and Clio's deterioration trend should not be flattened into one story: channel, sample, period and response definition differ. Neither is a randomized trial of AI. Independent press repeating Clio does not constitute an independent dataset. This bounded review did not establish a representative independent 2026 national response rate.

The practical unit is a firm's **unique eligible inquiry**, deduplicated across voice and email, followed through human contact, consultation, engagement and collected contribution. An unanswered inquiry may be spam, a conflict, the wrong jurisdiction, a matter the firm cannot handle, or a signal the firm lacks capacity. Investigate these alternatives before calling it lost revenue.

## The opportunity to test

The candidate advantage is implementation and operational accountability: a firm's actual scripts, dedicated inbox routing, a reliable transfer/callback process, safe escalation, staff training, integration repair and measured service levels. Voice plus email plus a portal is not, by itself, a defensible product gap in this market.

| Candidate segment | Why it might pay | Buyer and constraints | Initial stance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2–10 attorney estate-planning / small-business practice | Scheduled consultations; owner/office-manager workflow can be bounded | Managing partner approves; office manager operates; counsel/IT review. Sensitive family, asset and business data remain excluded at first contact. | Preferred hypothesis for discovery; verify volume and capacity |
| Employment / civil litigation boutiques | Potentially frequent mixed-channel inquiries and substantial screening work | Conflicts, deadlines, opposing parties and fact sensitivity; trained intake staff essential | Later, callback-only before any substantive intake |
| Personal injury, criminal, family, immigration | Urgent inquiries may make responsiveness valuable | Mature specialist competitors, distressed callers, health/criminal/safety information, emergency/deadline and language risks | Defer as first live pilot absent suitable expert operations |
| Solo firms with very low volume | Owner is reachable as buyer | Few observations, low spend tolerance, existing form plus human service may suffice | Often refer to/configure existing tools; avoid custom SaaS |
| Larger firms / corporate legal | Budget and volume | Procurement, identity, ethical walls, security reviews and bespoke integration increase sales cycle | Not first SMB pilot |

These are prioritization judgments, not verified demand or exclusion based on any person's protected characteristics. No firm list was assembled or contacted.

## Market sizing without fictional TAM

Start bottom-up after authorized discovery: count reachable firms in the chosen geography and practice, verify their monthly eligible inquiries, existing tooling, capacity, buyer ownership and willingness to pay. Serviceable annual revenue is **verified suitable firms × realistic adoption × annual contract value**, with each factor sourced or explicitly hypothetical. National attorney headcount is not a firm count. Multiplying all legal spend by a software percentage would not answer this product's market question.
