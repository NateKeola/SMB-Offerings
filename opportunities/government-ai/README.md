# Government AI implementation: diligence and entry plan

Prepared 2026-11-07. **Research and proposed validation only.** The linked carousel proposes selling AI implementation to government. This package evaluates that channel; it does not select it over the existing wholesale and local-service tracks.

## Assessment

**A real market, with a difficult sales and delivery boundary.** The best initial hypothesis is a narrowly scoped training, workflow-evaluation or implementation work package under an established contractor or a small public-sector buyer. Generic AI training and generic chatbot resale face strong substitutes. Large federal programs require capabilities and procurement access that this project has not established.

The carousel's spending numbers have a traceable research source, but are misleading if interpreted as annual 2024 versus 2026 spending. SAM registration is free; the official checklist says allow at least ten business days after submission, rather than promising seven to ten. The general federal purchase thresholds are current, with important exceptions. See [claims and market evidence](claims-and-market.md).

## Current evidence

A private research queue contains six source-backed entries: two workforce-board notices, including one AI training RFI and one adjacent website-support RFQ, plus four established contractor partner routes. Four additional records are held or excluded for expired responses, specialist eligibility or missing primary-source confirmation. These are research entries, **zero qualified buyers**. This bounded search found no suitable, fully verified open federal RFP ready for a new generalist agency to bid.

Buyer budgets, buying intent toward this firm, win probability, relevant past performance and delivery capacity remain unknown. No registration, outreach, bid, purchase, recurring monitor or implementation occurred.
