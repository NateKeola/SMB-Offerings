## Research method and limits

Exa searches covered post identity, market metrics, procurement rules, competitors, supplier routes and current notices. Direct source reads and an exact-URL web search resolved gaps; off-topic creator posts, expired notices and aggregator-only claims were not promoted to verified opportunities. Some indexed pages used AI or SI terminology; both were treated as source wording, not proof of capability.

The 12-slide summary from parallel browser inspection establishes the post's topic and proposal. The available evidence does not establish the creator's revenue, course results, typical award size, a new firm's win rate or a universal sales cycle. No national market-size estimate was reverse engineered from the carousel.
