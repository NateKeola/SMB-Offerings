# Pricing and unit economics

Prepared 2026-11-07. Every price, hour, cost and win probability below is a **planning assumption**, not a customer quote, market average, expected award or validated margin.

## Price from deliverables and full cost

A training engagement might include discovery, two role-specific sessions, public-data exercises, assessment and a handoff guide. A document-workflow evaluation might include a permitted corpus, baseline, error analysis, small read-only demo and acceptance report. Production integration, security authorization, records migration and ongoing operations are separate scope.

[GSA CALC+](https://buy.gsa.gov/pricing/qr/know-more) distinguishes fully burdened labor ceiling rates from prices paid. Use a matched role, experience, location and contract scope; the page-wide median is not a valid quote for this service. No matched awarded price or buyer willingness to pay was established here. Free [InnovateUS courses](https://innovate-us.org/course/responsible-ai-for-public-professionals/) are a real substitute for generic instruction.

## Illustrative scenarios

Use USD. Labor cost includes an assumed $100 per person-hour for delivery and business development. All direct costs, contingencies and probabilities are assumptions.

| Scenario | Revenue | Delivery hours | Other direct cost | Contingency | Delivery cost | Contribution before acquisition | Acquisition cost per win | Contribution after acquisition |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Training, low price | 6,000 | 36 | 400 | 500 | 4,500 | 1,500 (25.0%) | 2,400 | -900 (-15.0%) |
| Training, price test | 9,000 | 36 | 400 | 500 | 4,500 | 4,500 (50.0%) | 2,400 | 2,100 (23.3%) |
| Document evaluation, low price | 12,000 | 84 | 500 | 1,000 | 9,900 | 2,100 (17.5%) | 4,800 | -2,700 (-22.5%) |
| Document evaluation, price test | 20,000 | 84 | 500 | 1,000 | 9,900 | 10,100 (50.5%) | 4,800 | 5,300 (26.5%) |

Training delivery hours include 12 hours preparation plus 24 hours discovery, delivery, assessment and handoff. The document case includes 60 implementation/evaluation hours, 16 QA hours and eight project-management hours.

Training acquisition assumes six hours per pursuit and a 25% win probability: 6 × $100 / 0.25 = $2,400. The document case uses 12 hours: 12 × $100 / 0.25 = $4,800. This is a long-run planning allocation; it is not cash incurred in every individual sale and does not establish the firm's actual probability of winning.

Formula: delivery cost = labor hours × internal cost/hour + other direct costs + contingency. Contribution after acquisition = price − delivery cost − pursuit cost / win probability. Taxes, unallocated overhead, financing, insurance increases and unusual compliance work are excluded; add them before quoting.

At a modeled 20% margin after acquisition, price floors are $8,625 for training and $18,375 for document evaluation. These floors are not proof a buyer will pay. Prices must not be manipulated or requirements split to avoid procurement thresholds.

## Sensitivity and cash timing

| Win probability assumption | Training acquisition allocation | $9,000 training contribution | Document acquisition allocation | $20,000 document contribution |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10% | 6,000 | -1,500 | 12,000 | -1,900 |
| 25% | 2,400 | 2,100 | 4,800 | 5,300 |
| 50% | 1,200 | 3,300 | 2,400 | 7,700 |

A further 20 delivery hours costs $2,000 at the assumed rate. Model 30/60/90-day post-delivery receipt scenarios rather than treating award value as cash. In the price-test cases, one acquisition allocation plus delivery cost requires an illustrative $6,900 or $14,700 of capacity before receipt; accounting allocations and payroll cash are not identical. Actual contract acceptance, invoice terms, prime payment terms, retainage, taxes and available working capital remain unknown.

Do not sell an unlimited maintenance retainer. Define supported workflows, changes, model/hosting cost, incident coverage and capped support hours. Track real hours and bills during the first authorized pilot, then replace assumptions with evidence. A nominally cheap model does not make review, integration or procurement free.
