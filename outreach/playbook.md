# Calling-first outreach preparation

**Current state: no active campaign. Email sending is on hold.** This is an original proposed playbook assembled from the user's channel preferences and existing research. Scripts and timing below have not been tested and do not authorize calls, messages or visits.

## Before a conversation

Have a verified business/branch, a fresh public business route, reviewed task evidence, an honest demo status, a specific next question and a suppression check. Do not pitch an inferred problem as fact. Confirm the applicable jurisdiction and contact policy before a separately authorized campaign; this document is not legal clearance or a claim that public contact details equal consent.

Lead with the customer's task and a concrete observation. Explain what was checked, what was not, and the small improvement being explored. Avoid broad AI-transformation language, generic free-audit claims and unsupported lost-revenue estimates.

## Draft call structure

1. Identify yourself and the reason for the business call; ask for a brief conversation with the relevant role.
2. State one precise, reproducible public observation. If no issue was established, ask a discovery question without claiming one.
3. Ask how the team handles the task today, how often it occurs and whether it is worth improving.
4. Clarify capacity, current tools, decision process, usable data and the cost of change.
5. If the problem is recognized, offer a short bounded walkthrough with clearly labeled fictional data and scope. Agree a concrete next step only if useful.
6. Record the actual disposition, uncertainty, requested channel and any stop/no-contact instruction privately.

Example, to customize only when evidence supports it:

> I work on small improvements to product discovery and quote requests. While checking your public catalog, I could find the known item by its exact name, but the descriptive task I tested did not return it. I haven't seen your internal workflow. Is this something your sales team handles often, or is it already covered another way?

For a service business, replace the catalog claim with the actual observed service/inquiry task. Do not use a template claim where the test was inconclusive.

## Other channels: preparation only

| Channel | Useful role | Draft / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Email | A concise requested follow-up with evidence and demo scope | Hold all sending. Draft: observed task → question → what the demo shows → price/effort assumptions → one next step. No fabricated personalization or results. |
| LinkedIn | Verify a public professional role and prepare a relevant, human message | No automated connections/DMs, personal-data mining or messages without authorization. |
| In-person drop-in | A selective local conversation when suitable for the business | Respect business hours and staff workload; no appointment/relationship claim. Obtain authorization before visits are treated as an action plan. |

Do not infer an email address from a naming pattern and mark it verified. Verification services can have unknown/catch-all/invalid outcomes and do not establish identity, permission or deliverability.

## Proposed cadence for a later authorized pilot

Use one coherent account record across channels. A possible starting plan is: day 0 evidence review and first human call; day 3–5 one useful follow-up through an authorized route; day 10–14 one final attempt if appropriate; then close or hold. This is an untested maximum of three attempts, not a recommended legal safe harbor or scheduled automation. A requested response time overrides the template. Do not repeatedly retry a channel that is blocked or unwelcome.

Email remains excluded until the user changes the hold. No-response is not evidence of poor fit or poor website performance. Avoid stacking calls, email and social contact on the same day simply because they are available.

## Qualification questions and stop conditions

Ask about task frequency, current workaround, staff time, customer errors, capacity, integration ownership, existing vendor contract and a meaningful success measure. A firm that is fully booked or satisfied with its existing tools may have no useful offer. Stop when there is no reproducible issue, no buyer-recognized need, unavailable data, excessive delivery risk, no viable economics or a request not to contact.

Maintain suppression across later authorized campaigns and channels; the prototype's workspace-local mechanism is insufficient for multiuser operations. Never use a new profile or workspace to bypass it. The engine cannot send or call.

## Measurement

Track unique candidates, verified identities, assessable tasks, qualified opportunities, actual attempts by channel, reached decision-makers, recognized problems, walkthroughs, scoped proposals, paid pilots and delivered outcomes. Use actual denominators and dates. A click-to-call is not a connected call; an inquiry is not a qualified lead; a planned demo is not a built prototype; a proposal is not paid demand.

Measure staff time and fulfilled business value with the customer's agreed definitions. Record no-need, no-capacity and no-response outcomes rather than only wins. Include research, failed calls, demos and follow-up in acquisition cost. See [the pilot scorecard](../templates/pilot-scorecard.md).

## Discovery-to-demo reference — 2026-11-04

**Source:** [Service-business website workflow by @christian.wtc](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZYVM4WSMaC/), about 79 seconds. Reviewed public attribution, caption and sampled video frames on 2026-11-04 UTC. Audio was muted and the spoken explanation was not transcribed. Publication date unknown; no media or guide was downloaded.

**Observed:** Around 16–24 seconds, the creator shows searches for industry Facebook groups and an AI-generated list of home-service niches. Around 32–42 seconds, a service-business group and a sample conversation are shown; the conversation asks permission to send a website preview before discussing price. Around 45 seconds, a reusable service-site editor contains location placeholders and a quote-request form. These observations do not verify the conversation, a sale, a functioning form or qualified demand. The opening $30k/month headline and filmed revenue screen are unverified and do not establish profit, attribution or typical results.

**Useful adaptation, proposed rather than executed:**
1. Use niche lists and industry discussions to generate research questions, not a scraped contact list or evidence that members need a website.
2. Verify the business identity, current site and an actual customer task before claiming a gap. A missing website field or social-profile link is not proof of need.
3. Prepare one original, bounded preview only after the task is understood. Ask permission before sharing it through an authorized channel; label fictional content and unfinished behavior.
4. Reuse a small service-page structure, then check truthful service/location copy, mobile usability, quote fields, routing and response ownership.
5. Measure recognized problems, accepted walkthroughs, scoped proposals and paid delivery separately. Include research and unsuccessful demos in acquisition cost.

This reference does not select a vertical, authorize group scraping or outreach, or change the email hold. No group was joined, comment posted, guide requested or message sent. See the [reference index](../research/video-reference-index.md#additional-instagram-references--2026-11-04).

## Price-objection reference — 2026-11-04

**Source:** [Website-sales example by @pages.by.p](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd7FBlKpX59/), about 1 minute 54 seconds. Public caption and sampled frames were visually reviewed on 2026-11-04 UTC with audio muted; the final roughly 19 seconds were not reviewed. Publication date unknown. No complete call transcript, precise spoken script or sale outcome was verified.

**Observed:** Around 27–32 seconds, teaching overlays recommend asking what is behind an objection and learning earlier quotes and the buyer's budget. The caption interprets this prospect's resistance as a reaction to previous prices, not lack of interest; that is the creator's interpretation, not independently established buyer intent. Around 1:29–1:35, a map tool displays website/social-page/category filters and a prospect card with a confidence label and manual verification option. Tool identity, accuracy, data rights and contact permission were not verified.

**Useful adaptation:** Keep separate outcomes for no need, no capacity, poor timing, scope mismatch and unaffordable prior proposals. When a person is willing to discuss the issue, ask what the earlier offer included and what outcome matters before adjusting scope. A lower price is not automatically the answer. A clearly declined conversation is a stop signal, not an objection to overcome.

Use website-gap indicators only to prioritize verification. Resolve the exact business and current website, then test the customer task; missing-site confidence is not pain, consent or demand. Any later authorized proposal should explain its scope and costs without invented savings, urgency or social proof. No prospect list, contact details or call transcript is reproduced, and no outreach or tool use was initiated.

## AI site-preview reference — 2026-11-04

**Source:** [Maps-to-website demonstration by @idanbuild](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeKhK-kgjbv/), about 52 seconds. Public attribution, expanded caption and sampled frames across the reel were visually reviewed on 2026-11-04 UTC with audio muted. No complete transcript, guide or media download was obtained. Publication date unknown.

**Observed:** Around 9–15 seconds, a niche-and-city Google Maps search shows a listing without a visible Website button. Around 19–25 seconds, the creator moves to Webild, uses its prompt area and shows a generated preview; the exact submitted prompt was not captured clearly enough to reproduce. Around 28–35 seconds, the preview contains large imagery and property cards. Around 46 seconds, a publish dialog includes a domain-search field. A missing listing button does not establish that a business lacks a site, and a publish dialog does not establish deployment or customer approval.

**Useful adaptation:** Verify whether a site is absent, broken or merely unlinked before proposing work. Build an original private concept around a confirmed customer task, with fictional or authorized content. Before any authorized delivery, check contact/service facts, image rights, mobile and keyboard usability, working enquiry routes, domain ownership and approval. For property-related examples, do not invent inventory, availability, testimonials or affiliations; AI-generated cards are not verified listings. Label the concept and its simulated behavior clearly.

The $1m website-sales headline and caption are unverified promotional claims. The reel does not establish revenue, pricing, rankings, traffic, qualified leads, conversion or a completed client sale. Local SEO and ongoing care would need separately scoped work and evidence rather than being assumed benefits of the builder. No business was contacted and no website was built or published during this review.
