### Evidence and limits

- **Official guidance:** Google's [generative AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide?version=published), rechecked on 2026-11-08, supports useful original content and ordinary SEO foundations. It does not require special AI files/schema, artificial chunking or a particular writing formula. Eligibility includes the applicable Search Console inclusion control and does not guarantee display. This is Google-specific, not a rule for every assistant. See historical source entries S32 and S62 for context.
- **Vendor observation, not a target score:** [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/why-chatgpt-cites-pages/) reports title-to-prompt similarity of 0.602 for cited pages versus 0.484 for uncited pages in its 1.4-million-prompt study. Its embedding-based approximation does not reveal the model's internal ranking formula or establish that a title edit causes citations.
- **Population-specific evidence:** [Shopify's Q2 2026 analysis](https://www.shopify.com/au/enterprise/blog/ai-search-category-behavior) reports AI-referred sessions up 197% and organic sessions up 12% year over year. These are Shopify storefront observations, not expected results for a local service business or proof that AI never displaces traffic.
- **Technical qualification:** [Vercel/MERJ's crawler research](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler) supports exposing important content without depending on client-side JavaScript. Its observations are scoped to the studied crawlers and period, not every future assistant or browser agent. Verify the actual retrieval path.
- **Measurement clarification:** [GA4 documents both engagement and bounce rate](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/12195621?hl=en-419). Bounce rate is the percentage of non-engaged sessions, not the old Universal Analytics definition.

Question-form headings and three-click navigation remain possible design heuristics, not adopted ranking requirements. Other quoted percentage claims are not promoted into this toolkit as universal benchmarks; the exact declarative-opening uplift was not independently established in this review. No target-site audit, analytics access or citation-lift experiment occurred.

### Proposed SMB application

Choose one commercially important page and one real buyer task. Record its factual answer, distinctive evidence, content accessibility, next action and qualified-inquiry baseline. Fix the strongest verified gap, document the change and measure afterward with explicit attribution limits. Reuse the [existing audit method](../research/idea-mining/seo-audit.md) and [search playbook](search-and-ai-visibility.md); do not create a second score, force a format, or promise ranking, citation or revenue gains.
